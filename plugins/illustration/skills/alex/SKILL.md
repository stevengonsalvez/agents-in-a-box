# illustration:alex — ShotClubhouse Sport Head

Generate brand and explanatory illustrations starring **Alex**, ShotClubhouse's trademarked Sport Head character. Alex turns a message, product flow, or sporting moment into a bold comic-style image with confident street-hero energy: *take your shot, own it, make impact.*

## Core positioning

Alex is a person whose head is a glossy golden-and-black soccer ball. He is cool, purposeful, and aspirational — never goofy. The brand is sport identity for everyone: clubs, coaches, athletes, parents; grassroots to stadium; verified, safe, global. Humor and warmth come from real-sport moments and swagger, not from silly faces.

## Workflow (engine + Alex specifics)

Follow the five shared steps. Alex specifics:

- **Likeness is mandatory:** pass `assets/alex-ref/alex-source.png` into the backend every time with "this is Alex; draw THIS exact character, translated into bold comic streetwear; do not copy the reference background."
- **Two registers** (see style DNA): *faithful comic* for hero/social/marketing; the same look stays readable for explainer/how-it-works by simplifying the scene, not the character.
- **On-brand anchors of meaning:** the street-to-stadium arc, the Sport Head ID, the four pillars (club/coach/athlete/parent), "TAKE YOUR SHOT / OWN IT / MAKE IMPACT".
- Keep ShotClubhouse colorways: gold, black, purple, with electric accent lighting.

## Output style

Pre-generation strategy: short and punchy. Delivery report: how many, what each is for, save paths, which are hero-grade vs optional. Let the art carry it.
