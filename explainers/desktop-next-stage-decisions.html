01

What we want, and the shape that gets there

One UI drives N boxes and streams them live. A phone answers a needs-input banner in two taps. Every surface reads one agent status. We are rearchitecting ground-up, so these are the decisions to take now.

04

Nine decisions

Recommended option first, in green. Bars are judgment, not measurement: fit to the feature map, cost (5 = cheap), risk (5 = safe). Every option carries its own downside.

X1

tmux: keep, replace, or hybrid?

Hybrid. tmux stays PTY owner and multi-attach substrate. The daemon adds a headless VT emulator fed by the attached stream, giving snapshot and status without replacing tmux.

tmux ──attach (portable-pty)──▶ daemon VT emulator ──▶ snapshot ──▶ phone first frame
  └── N viewers free · survives our restart · operator can attach by hand
Recommended

Hybrid

tmux owns the PTY. Daemon emulator for snapshot, per-viewer flow control, and tier-2 status.

We parse tmux's re-render, not the child's bytes. Alt-screen, mouse, OSC 8 may not survive the hop. Spike 2 decides.

cost3
risk3
Alternative

Daemon-owned PTY

Replace tmux with our own PTY host and emulator, like the reference.

Loses discovery of hand-started agents and survival across our own restart. The reference's daemon dies on a service restart. Eight new persistence modules.

cost1
risk2
Alternative

tmux only

No emulator. Keep capture-pane as the terminal view.

capture-pane cannot express cursor, alt-screen, mouse mode, or link ranges. No authoritative first frame for a phone. This is the hard mobile blocker.

  • Input and resize arbitration between two typists: not built until a phone can type. [inference]
X2

Host model: who owns the control plane on a remote box?

Peer daemon on the box. Same binary, own control plane, HostId on every wire type. ssh -L becomes one carrier among LAN and tailnet, not the architecture.

laptop ──WS+E2EE──▶ daemon on box (owns sessions) ◀── laptop closed, agents keep running
   carriers: tailnet · LAN · ssh -L   (any of them, same wire)
Recommended

Peer daemon + HostId

Box owns sessions. Socket path versioned by protocol, never by build. Listings fan out with a coverage census.

Every listing becomes a fan-out. Without omitted_hosts a host silently vanishes under a row cap.

fit5
cost3
risk3
Alternative · spec wording today

Client-owned over ssh -L

Desktop owns the forwarded socket; remote is "the same socket, further away".

Nothing on the box works while the laptop is shut. A socket path tied to a build orphans live work on every update.

fit2
cost5
risk2
Alternative

Both models per box

Register a box as a dumb ssh host and as a peer.

Splits its sessions across two identities. Listings depend on which flag you passed.

fit3
cost4
risk1

  • HostId through 159 proto methods, fleet_session, three clients: 1.5k LOC plus a migration, 2-3 weeks. Rewrites the D4 transport row. [inference]

Recommended

No relay

Zero build, zero service. Pairing offer carries an empty relay slot.

A phone on cellular with no VPN profile cannot reach the box at all.

X4

Off-box transport and auth

WebSocket carrying the existing JSON-RPC envelope, application-level E2EE, per-device revocable tokens with a scope, scope-to-method allowlist checked before dispatch.

pair (QR · deep link · paste) ──▶ { endpoints[], device_token, host_pubkey, scope, relay? }
connect ──▶ X25519 handshake, transcript binds transport + host id ──▶ AEAD frames
dispatch ──▶ scope → method allowlist BEFORE handler
Recommended

WS + E2EE + device tokens

Host key pinned in the offer, so TLS is optional and a relay can be untrusted later.

Tokens have no expiry in the reference; revocation is the only control. Add expiry ourselves.

X5

Agent status: signal hierarchy and where the store lives

Six tiers, one daemon-owned store, single writer, provenance stamped at write time. Only hooks and structured feeds may open a turn or assert needs-input. Silence never becomes done.

T0 hook push ─┐  only T0/T1 may OPEN a turn or assert needs-input
T1 ACP feed  ─┤
T2 OSC frame ─┼──▶ daemon store, single writer, provenance per row ──▶ every surface mirrors
T3 process   ─┤
T4 transcript─┤  usage and prompt recovery only
T5 pane text ─┘  discovery of hand-started agents · readiness gate · tagged last-resort row
silence ──▶ unverifiable (we hold the pane) | idle (we do not).  never done
Recommended

Six tiers, daemon store

Three clocks per row. restored_unconfirmed on hydrate. Tiers 0, 1, 3, 4 are tmux-independent.

Per-provider normalizers are ongoing work. One hookless CLI cost the reference 300 lines and 75 hardcoded status words.

Shared literal lists strand providers that name events differently. Pinned to a CLI's rendering, breaks on its updates.

Each surface derives status from the raw feeds.

Three copies keyed differently. One session reads differently on desktop, phone, and CLI. The reference is mid-refactor out of exactly this.

Renderer contract under the fan-out measurements

Keep plan B, the whole-state mirror in 19 versioned sections, with three day-one invariants. Frames name changed sections. One store transaction per channel drain, effects after commit. Clients may subscribe to a subset.

Section naming, per-drain apply, subscription filter. About 400 LOC plus a listener census with a CI ceiling.

Naive mirroring measured 9,279 listeners, 160 publications/s, 11.6 points of renderer CPU, with zero long tasks to warn you.

Integer + capabilities

A catalogue of 65-80 strings to maintain. Cheapest item today, most expensive after a phone ships.

X9

Idempotent mutation envelope

Every mutation carries a client-minted op id and an expected fence. The daemon commits first, then replies. A retry gets the committed result.

client mints op_id (ts-prefixed, freshness-bounded) + expected fence
daemon commits FIRST, then replies ──▶ retry gets: created | adopted | replayed
receipt marks the pre-PTY-write boundary; after the write, effects are ambiguous
Recommended

Op id + fence + receipt

Generalises the locked "reject-second on answers" into the envelope for all mutations.

900 LOC plus a migration; answer.rs is 1,940 lines to thread through.

First-answer-wins only

SQLite conditional update on answers, nothing else.

A lost reply cannot be told from not-delivered. A retry double-fires "approve" on an agent about to run a command. Correctness bug, not UX.

Retained ids must be expiry-bounded, or a dropped id becomes a second message on the host.

07

Spikes before committing

Spikes 1 and 4 can run during P0+S. None blocks the keymap or Phase S work.

1 · OSC through tmux

Emit an OSC status frame from a wrapper inside a tmux pane with allow-passthrough on. Does it reach a portable-pty attach stream intact? On failure, hooks carry everything alone over tmux.

1 day · flips X5 tier 2

2 · Snapshot fidelity over the tmux hop

Drive alt-screen, mouse, wide chars, OSC 8 through tmux attach under portable-pty into a Rust VT emulator. Diff the snapshot against a direct PTY. Poor fidelity reopens the daemon-owned PTY.

2-3 days · flips X1

3 · Peer WS + E2EE prototype

Over tailnet and over ssh -L. Confirm the daemon keeps working after the desktop closes. Measure reconnect and resync.

2-3 days · go for R1 before D4 rescope

4 · Fan-out on the real 19 sections

Extend the planned specta spike: 2,000-update burst at 100 sessions through the Solid store with a listener census. Per-section sends versus one transaction per drain.

2 days · flips X6
